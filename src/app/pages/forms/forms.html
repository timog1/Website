Two Approaches to Forms

Angular provides two paradigms for handling forms. Choose the right one based on your use case:

📝 Template-Driven Forms

Simple forms where the logic lives in the template. Uses directives like ngModel and template variables. Best for simple forms with basic validation.

  • • Less boilerplate code
  • • Easier to write for simple forms
  • • Harder to test
  • • Asynchronous validation

⚡ Reactive Forms

Model-driven approach where form structure is defined in the component class. Best for complex forms with dynamic validation. Recommended for most cases.

  • • More explicit and testable
  • • Better for complex forms
  • • Synchronous validation
  • • Dynamic form controls
